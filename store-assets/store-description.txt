NAME
Creamy Sky Theme

SUMMARY
A bright Chrome theme blending creamy yellow, soft ivory, and clear sky blue for a sunny calm browsing space.

DESCRIPTION
Creamy Sky Theme brings a little sunshine to Chrome. A clear sky-blue frame pairs with a creamy yellow toolbar and active tab, while warm ivory keeps your new tab page light and calm. Blue-gray text and icons keep the interface clear and easy to read.

A simple flat sun-and-cloud icon captures the bright, airy palette. Solid colors throughout, with no wallpaper, no extra scripts, and no permissions required.

PROMO
A little sunshine in every tab.

MARQUEE
Blue skies. Warm cream.
A little sunshine in every tab.
